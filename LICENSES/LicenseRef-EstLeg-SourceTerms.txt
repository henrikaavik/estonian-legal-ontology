Third-party source terms (Estonian Legal Ontology)

Files carrying this identifier contain, alongside the project's own
compilation layer (CC-BY-4.0), verbatim or lightly transformed third-party
legal material that the project did not author and cannot licence:

- Estonian statutes, regulations and draft legislation from Riigi Teataja
  (https://www.riigiteataja.ee) and the Eelnõude infosüsteem
  (https://eelnoud.valitsus.ee). The official texts are not objects of
  copyright under § 5 of the Estonian Copyright Act (Autoriõiguse seadus).
  The consolidated product and any database right remain subject to the
  publisher's terms.
- Judgments of the Estonian Supreme Court (Riigikohus, via the RIK
  court-decision database). Not objects of copyright under the same § 5, but
  they contain personal data; republication is governed by data-protection
  law.
- European Union material (EUR-Lex acts, CURIA decisions, EuroVoc).
  © European Union, 1998–2026, reused under Commission Decision 2011/833/EU
  of 12 December 2011 on the reuse of Commission documents.

Each source keeps its own terms. They are set out in full, with the open
verification items, in the NOTICE file at the root of this repository
("PER-SOURCE RIGHTS STATEMENTS") and explained in docs/DATA_RIGHTS.md.
Personal-data handling is described in docs/DATA_PROTECTION.md. A reuser
must satisfy both these source terms and CC-BY-4.0 for the compilation
layer.
